1. The Problem: The Exploding Crisis of Fake Job Offers

Every year, millions of college graduates, interns, and eager job seekers receive exciting emails congratulating them on landing a job. But far too often, these offers are devastating traps.

Over 82% of students report encountering fake recruiters online. Scammers use realistic-looking company logos, professional letterheads, and convincing emails to trick young candidates into two severe traps:

1. Upfront Money Extortion (Advance Fees)

The scammer demands ₹2,000 to ₹15,000 as a "refundable laptop courier fee", "training material fee", or "mandatory background check deposit" via personal Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm UPI handles. Once paid, the recruiter vanishes.

2. Identity Theft (Premature KYC Harvesting)

Before any real interview, scammers demand clear color scans of Aadhaar cards, PAN cards, bank passbooks, and signed blank cheques. These documents are then used to open fraudulent bank accounts and take out illegal loans in the student's name.

2. Why Existing Solutions Fail

When students ask their placement cells or IT departments for help, they find that traditional security tools simply weren't built for this problem:

  • Standard Email Spam Filters (Gmail/Outlook): Email filters only check if the sending mail server is configured properly (SPF/DKIM). Scammers easily bypass them by using free Gmail accounts (@gmail.com) or freshly registered domains. Traditional filters understand network packets, not employment law.
  • Using Cloud LLMs (ChatGPT / Gemini): Asking an external cloud AI to read every offer letter sounds modern, but it causes three fatal flaws in practice:
    • Severe Latency: Cloud APIs take 2 to 5 seconds per file, making batch processing hundreds of students impossible.
    • Massive Cost: Scanning thousands of letters costs thousands of dollars in recurring token fees.
    • Privacy Violations: Uploading student resumes, home addresses, and government IDs to external third-party cloud servers violates strict data privacy laws like India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act.

5. What Happens Behind the Scenes: The Forensic Layers

When the user drops a file or when an email arrives, what actually happens inside the server? Here is the exact technical journey:

Layer 1: Document Ingestion & On-Device OCR INPUT PROCESSING

The file enters our FastAPI backend as raw bytes in temporary RAM memory. If it is a PDF, pypdf reads the text streams and hidden creation metadata. If it is an image, VeriHire calls Apple's native CoreML Vision Neural Engine running directly on the Mac's hardware (with a seamless fallback to Tesseract on Linux). OCR finishes in under 3 milliseconds without sending a single byte outside the machine.

Layer 2: Candidate Privacy & PII Scrubbing PRIVACY SHIELD

Before any analysis begins, our privacy engine masks candidate personal data in memory using regex: Aadhaar numbers become [REDACTED_AADHAAR], PAN numbers become [REDACTED_PAN], and phone numbers are scrubbed. This guarantees candidate identities remain protected throughout the pipeline.

Layer 3: The Six Heuristic Threat Engines FORENSIC EVALUATION
The sanitized text is cross-referenced across 6 independent threat vectors:
• Vector 1 (AI & Template Fingerprints): Catches unfilled template placeholders ([Candidate Name], <Company Name>) and repetitive AI language tropes ("elevate your resume", "passionate cohort").
• Vector 2 (Social Media Lures): Flags cold outreach claiming "We found your profile on Instagram / Telegram" and offers issued without technical interviews.
• Vector 3 (Advance-Fee Traps): Detects illegal charges ("laptop dispatch deposit", "training fee") and catches personal UPI handles (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm).
• Vector 4 (Premature Identity Theft): Penalizes demands for color scans of Aadhaar/PAN or signed blank cheques over email.
• Vector 5 (Domain Spoofing & Typosquatting): Flags official corporate letters sent from free accounts (@gmail.com) or lookalike domains targeting 17 major tech brands (e.g. google-portal.xyz, tcs-hiring.live).
• Vector 6 (PDF Creation Software): Checks PDF metadata to catch letters designed in consumer graphics tools (Canva, Photoshop) rather than enterprise HR systems (SAP, Workday).
Layer 4: Anti-False-Positive Shield (Legitimacy Credits) BALANCE CHECK

To ensure genuine corporate offers are never mistakenly flagged, VeriHire applies score discounts when authentic enterprise markers exist: statutory Indian payroll structures (EPF, HRA, Gratuity, CTC Annexures), verified enterprise portal links (e.g., careers.google.com, workday.com), and official Government Corporate Identity Numbers (CIN).

Layer 5: Dynamic Mathematical Risk Scoring FINAL VERDICT

The threat penalties and legitimacy credits calculate a final Risk Score between 0 and 100:
• 0 to 29: LEGITIMATE_OFFER_PROFILE (Safe / Verified)
• 30 to 64: SUSPICIOUS_REVIEW_REQUIRED (Needs Placement Cell Review)
• 65 to 100: MALICIOUS_OFFER_SCAM (Critical Threat — Do not pay or share IDs)

7. Storage of Files: Absolute Zero-Data Retention

One of the most important questions judges ask is: "Where do the files go, and how do you protect student privacy?"

Our Strict Zero-Storage Guarantee:

VeriHire stores zero files on disk or persistent databases. Everything is processed as temporary byte buffers (io.BytesIO) directly in computer RAM and wiped the moment the analysis finishes. Any temporary image slices created during OCR are automatically deleted using os.remove() inside strict finally blocks. The system complies 100% with India's DPDP Act 2023 and Europe's GDPR.
