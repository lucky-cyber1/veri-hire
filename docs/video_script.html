Your Answer: "Great question! Using Gmail only adds fifteen penalty points. In VeriHire, a document needs thirty points to be suspicious and sixty-five points to be a scam. A legitimate startup offer letter that doesn't demand advance fees, doesn't steal identity cards, and doesn't use lookalike domains will easily stay in the safe green zone."

Judge: "Can a scammer bypass this by rewriting the email using ChatGPT?"
Your Answer: "No, because we evaluate multiple independent structural layers. Even if a scammer polishes their wording, they still have to ask for the money or IDs, their sender domain will still fail authentication, and their document will lack valid corporate registration tokens. They cannot hide across all six layers simultaneously."
