4. Quick Judge Q&A Defense "Cheat Sheet"

Q: Why not just use GPT-4 or Gemini for this?
A: "Cloud LLMs cost $0.02 to $0.05 per document, take 2 to 4 seconds to respond, hallucinate inconsistent scores, and violate DPDP privacy by sending candidate Aadhaar cards to external cloud servers. VeriHire runs locally in under 5 milliseconds at zero cost with 100% data sovereignty."

Q: What happens if a scammer changes their email wording?
A: "VeriHire doesn't just match keywords—it cross-references multiple independent structural vectors: domain typosquatting against 17 enterprise registries, PDF toolchain creation metadata, statutory payroll mathematics (EPF/HRA deductions), and peer-to-peer payment rails. Scammers cannot bypass all structural layers simultaneously."

Q: How do you prevent false positives on genuine offers?
A: "We engineered an Anti-False-Positive Shield that applies legitimacy credit discounts when authentic enterprise markers are present—such as valid Ministry of Corporate Affairs CIN registration numbers, statutory PF/Gratuity annexures, and verified enterprise ATS portal links."
