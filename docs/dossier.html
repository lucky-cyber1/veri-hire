1. Executive Summary & The Problem Statement

Recruitment fraud has escalated into a multi-billion dollar organized syndicate crisis. Over 82% of college students and early-career job seekers report receiving suspicious job offers, bogus internship appointments, or phishing recruitment lures. These syndicates execute three devastating attack vectors:

1. Advance-Fee Extortion

Coercing candidates into paying "refundable security deposits", "laptop courier dispatch fees", or "mandatory training charges" via personal UPI IDs (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm).

2. Premature Identity Harvesting

Demanding high-resolution scans of Government Aadhaar cards, PAN cards, bank account passbooks, and signed blank cheques under the guise of "pre-onboarding KYC".

Why Existing Solutions Fail:
  • Standard Email Filters (SPF/DKIM): Legitimate scammers register fresh domains or abuse legitimate free webmail accounts (`@gmail.com`) that pass DKIM/SPF checks cleanly.
  • Third-Party Cloud LLMs (OpenAI/Gemini): Introducing cloud LLMs creates critical latency (>2,000ms), prohibitive per-document API token costs, and catastrophic data privacy violations under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act by shipping candidate resumes and identity documents to external servers.

3. How It Works: The 6-Layer Forensic Engine

Every document or email ingested by VeriHire passes through a strictly sequenced forensic inspection pipeline:

Vector 1: LLM Stylometric & Template Fingerprinting +30 - +40 PTS

Scammers frequently use ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude to draft fake letters. VeriHire identifies unfilled template placeholders ([Candidate Name], <Insert Company>, [Salary / CTC]), AI model disclaimers ("As an AI language model..."), and generic generative tropes ("elevate your resume", "passionate cohort", "valuable addition").

Vector 2: Unsolicited Social Media Sourcing & Interview Bypass +25 - +35 PTS

Detects cold outreach exploiting platform authority: "We found your profile on Instagram / LinkedIn / Telegram and were impressed by your merits." Flags immediate job extensions issued without technical interviews ("direct selection without interview", "no technical round needed").

Vector 3: Advance-Fee Extortion & Peer-to-Peer UPI Traps +40 PTS (CRITICAL)

Scans for mandatory upfront fees: "refundable security deposit", "laptop courier dispatch fee", "onboarding software license", or "training certificate fee". Traps peer-to-peer payment rails (UPI handles, QR codes, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm).

Vector 4: Premature KYC & Digital Identity Harvesting +25 PTS

Enterprise employers collect PAN and Aadhaar records through authenticated employee portals (e.g. Workday) after formal acceptance. VeriHire penalizes requests demanding high-resolution front-and-back scans of Aadhaar/PAN or signed blank cheques via email.

Vector 5: Domain Spoofing, Free Webmail & Typosquatting +35 PTS

Cross-references sender domain against known enterprise registries. Flags official corporate offers sent from free webmail providers (@gmail.com, @yahoo.com, @proton.me) or lookalike domains targeting 17 monitored brands (e.g., google-careers-portal.xyz, tcs-hiring.live).

Vector 6: PDF Creation Toolchain Forensics +15 PTS

Inspects internal PDF Producer and Creator metadata. Flags letters generated via consumer graphic software (Canva, Smallpdf, iLovePDF, Photoshop) rather than enterprise ERP platforms (SAP SuccessFactors, Oracle HCM, Workday).

Anti-False-Positive Shield (Legitimacy Credit Offsets)

To ensure authentic corporate letters are never misclassified, VeriHire rewards legitimate structural markers:

  • Statutory Indian Payroll Components: Recognizes formal statutory deduction annexures (EPF, HRA, Gratuity, Special Allowance, Professional Tax) → -15 pts offset.
  • Enterprise ATS Verification: Authenticated links to corporate portals (careers.google.com, workday.com, greenhouse.io, docusign.net) → -15 pts offset.
  • Statutory Government Corporate Identifiers: Valid Ministry of Corporate Affairs Corporate Identity Numbers (CIN: 21 alphanumeric chars) and GSTIN tax patterns → -10 pts offset.
Dynamic Risk Scoring Formula:
Risk Score = Clamp(0, 100, Σ(Threat Penalties) - Σ(Legitimacy Offsets))

4. Zero-Retention Security & Data Privacy Architecture

Because recruitment documents contain the most sensitive personal data of candidates (names, home addresses, government IDs, phone numbers), VeriHire was architected under a strict Zero-Data-Retention mandate:

Component Architectural Implementation Regulatory Compliance
Document Ingestion Files processed purely as ephemeral byte buffers (io.BytesIO) in volatile RAM. No file copies are ever written to disk or databases. DPDP Act 2023 (Purpose Limitation)
OCR Temporary Frames Any cropped bitmap images created for Apple Vision OCR are atomically unlinked (os.remove()) inside strict finally blocks. GDPR Art. 17 (Right to Erasure)
PII Sanitization Candidate Aadhaar, PAN, and contact numbers are masked in RAM via regex before heuristic analysis commences. Data Minimization Principle
Mailbox Stream MailGuard keeps fetched email headers in a circular Python memory queue (emailsQueue). Restarting the process clears all data instantly. Volatile Ephemeral Footprint
