How VeriHire Works & PKI Explained

A plain-English guide to Public Key Infrastructure and document authenticity.

What Does "PKI Validated" Mean?

PKI (Public Key Infrastructure) is the global standard for cryptographic digital signatures. When genuine companies like Google, Microsoft, TCS, Infosys, or Accenture issue an offer letter, their authorized HR executive signs the PDF with a verified private key certified by a trusted Certificate Authority (e.g. Adobe Approved Trust List, eMudhra, DigiCert).

How to Tell If a Document Is PKI Validated

  • In Adobe Acrobat Reader: A legitimate corporate offer shows a top ribbon stating "Signed and all signatures are valid" with certificate details and signer identity.
  • In VeriHire: Our engine scans the PDF's cryptographic dictionary for x509 PKI certificates. If genuine, it awards the PKI VALIDATED badge with zero risk penalty.
  • Why Scammers Fail PKI: Fraudsters generate letters using Canva, Microsoft Word, or ChatGPT. They cannot forge cryptographic private keys, resulting in UNSIGNED / COUNTERFEIT status.
