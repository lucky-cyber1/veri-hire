PKI Architecture & Digital Signatures

Cryptographic verification standards for enterprise offer letters.

What Is PKI Validation?

Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) uses asymmetric cryptography to bind an organization’s identity to a digital document. Legitimate enterprises (Google, TCS, Accenture, Microsoft) digitally sign PDF appointment letters using accredited Certificate Authorities (Adobe AATL / eMudhra / DigiCert).

Deterministic Signatures vs Counterfeit Templates

  • In Adobe Acrobat: A valid document shows a top ribbon stating "Signed and all signatures are valid" with cryptographic certificate thumbprints.
  • In VeriHire: The engine audits the raw PDF byte structure for x509 certificate dictionaries, flagging documents as PKI VALIDATED.
  • Counterfeit Documents: Scammers drafting letters via Canva, Word, or LLM generators lack private keys, triggering an UNSIGNED / COUNTERFEIT status.
