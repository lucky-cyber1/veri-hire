How VeriHire Works & PKI Explained

Simple, easy-to-understand explanation of digital certificates.

What Is a "PKI Validated" Document?

PKI (Public Key Infrastructure) is like a tamper-proof digital wax seal. Real employers (Google, TCS, Accenture, Microsoft) digitally sign PDF offer letters using cryptographic keys issued by accredited government authorities (like eMudhra or DigiCert).

How to Tell If an Offer Is Real

  • In Adobe Acrobat Reader: A legitimate corporate offer shows a blue ribbon at the top stating "Signed and all signatures are valid".
  • In VeriHire: Our system inspects the PDF's cryptographic signature dictionary. Real offers earn the PKI VALIDATED badge.
  • Why Scammers Get Flagged: Scammers draft fake letters using Canva, Microsoft Word, or AI tools. They cannot generate accredited corporate digital signatures, so VeriHire flags them as UNSIGNED / COUNTERFEIT.
